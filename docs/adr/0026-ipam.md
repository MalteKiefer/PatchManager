# 26. IP address management

* Status: accepted
* Date: 2026-10-22

## Context

Documenting racks, ports and cables without the addresses used on them leaves the
network documentation incomplete. Operators need prefixes per site and VLAN, the
addresses on device ports, free space and the next free address.

## Decision

* New app `ipam` with `VRF`, `Prefix` (PostgreSQL `cidr`) and `IPAddress` (`inet`
  with its prefix length). Containment uses the native operators (`<<`, `<<=`,
  `>>`, `>>=`) through lookups on the custom fields, with GiST indexes.
* A prefix is scoped to its site or the tenant, an address to the device of its
  port or the tenant; the scope is stored in `path` so lists use the policy engine
  in SQL. Row Level Security and triggers keep VRF, site, VLAN and port in the
  row's tenant and the gateway inside its prefix.
* Addresses are unique per VRF and host. Containers count the space of their child
  prefixes, other prefixes their addresses; IPv4 network and broadcast addresses
  are not offered except in /31 and /32.
* Changes are tracked in the history (and so reach webhooks), audited, searchable,
  importable and exportable, and available in the REST API.
* Sites with prefixes or with addresses on their ports cannot move to another tenant
  until those are moved or removed, like VLANs.
* Not in this version: IP ranges, DHCP scopes, NAT, DNS integration.

## Consequences

* Moving a device re-scopes its addresses.
* The prefix list computes usage in Python; very large tenants may need it in SQL.
